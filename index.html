01 Zero-Monetization Covenant

Complete immunity from advertisement networks and consumer profiling

Finora was founded on an uncompromised foundational axiom: your personal finances are sacred and private. Finora has never sold, rented, monetized, or brokered customer transactional records, net worth assessments, recurring obligations, or financial behavioral data to third-party data brokers, advertising exchanges, or credit rating agencies.

The Sovereign Principle: Mainstream consumer finance tools monetize user attention, scrape credit cards for behavioral ads, or sell aggregated purchase streams. Finora’s economic model relies exclusively on sovereign software licensing. You are the customer, never the product.

We do not inject promotional sponsored banners, affiliate product recommendations, or behavioral tracking beacons into your ledger. What you earn, spend, allocate, and save remains strictly confidential between you and your encrypted vault.

02 Dual-Layer Cryptographic Vault Architecture

End-to-end transport isolation and persistent ledger protection

Every transaction, upcoming bill obligation, category budget, and savings target processed through Finora is protected by a multi-tiered security perimeter:

In-Flight (TLS 1.3)

Elliptic-curve cipher suites guarding all browser-to-server exchanges against eavesdropping and tampering.

At-Rest (AES-256)

Sovereign file and relational records written to persistent volumes with AES-256-GCM symmetric block encryption.

Salted Hashing (bcrypt)

Passwords undergo multi-round salted bcrypt hashing (work factor 10). Plaintext passwords are never retained.

Internal service workers operate under strict principle of least privilege (PoLP), ensuring that automated workers calculate liquidity indexes and balance forecasts without exposing raw unmasked telemetry.

03 Multi-Currency Ledger Isolation

Strict tenant compartmentalization for global and regional currency holdings

Finora seamlessly computes balances across Pakistani Rupee (PKR), US Dollar (USD), Euro (EUR), British Pound (GBP), UAE Dirham (AED), and Saudi Riyal (SAR). Exchange rate benchmarks are ingested strictly from public clearing houses and sovereign central bank open indices.

No third-party rate provider receives your account identity, account balance, or transaction details when conversion matrices are calculated. The conversion index is applied locally within your isolated tenant workspace.

04 Ephemeral Cookies & Strict Token Governance

Defending your session against Cross-Site Scripting (XSS) and CSRF attacks

Authentication in Finora employs hardened JSON Web Tokens (JWT) signed using high-entropy secrets and dispatched via HttpOnly, SameSite=Lax browser cookies, complemented by secure client-side storage for preview sandboxes.

Immediate Revocation: Clicking "Sign Out" from the top navigation or settings drawer instantly clears stored tokens and signals the server to invalidate the session cookie. There are zero persistent background trackers.

05 Unconditional Data Portability & Right to Erasure

Your ledger is always exportable and permanently deletable at any second

We believe data lock-in is a violation of financial dignity. At any point, you have the right to:

  • Instant CSV Export: Generate standardized RFC-4180 compliant CSV files of all transactions, category budgets, upcoming bills, and milestones with 1 click.
  • Complete Cryptographic Erasure: Delete your profile, credentials, and ledger permanently. Our deletion routine wipes entries with zero retention backups.
